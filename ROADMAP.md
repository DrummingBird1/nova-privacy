# Nova Homepage Roadmap

🌐 עברית: ראו את [README.he.md](README.he.md) לתיאור הפרויקט. מפת הדרכים להלן באנגלית.

A high-level look at where Nova Homepage has been and where it may go. Ideas are not promises, and nothing here has a date.

## Shipped

| Version | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| 1.x | First releases: the new-tab page, themes, links, search and notes, with all data stored on your device. |
| 2.0 | A larger feature release that rolled up the 1.4–1.7 work. |
| 3.0 | Dashboard builder, productivity hub (tasks, habits, planner, insights), on-device smart features, and 18 interface languages. |
| 3.0.1 | Bug-fix release: sync, layout, accessibility and reliability fixes. No new permissions. |

## Ideas being considered

- Todo shortcut words for more languages.
- Native-speaker review of translations.
- Layout polish for small browser windows.
- Per-profile background images.
- A print-friendly day card.
- A Firefox port.

## Not planned

Nova stays account-free and server-free, so these are out of scope:

- Broad website access for feeds or calendars.
- Cloud backup through third-party sign-in.

Feedback and ideas are welcome through issues on this repository.
